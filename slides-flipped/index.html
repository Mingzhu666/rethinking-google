The problem

Gemini can now build your deck in a minute. Understanding it still takes you.

Rehearsal tools today listen to you and grade you — pace, filler words, eye contact. They never test the harder thing: do you know what each slide is supposed to say? And their feedback is a score at the end, not a moment you can go back to.

"Isn't the AI supposed to save me work?"

It still builds the deck. Flipped makes sure the minute it saved doesn't cost you the room — ten minutes of auditing replaces an hour of reading your slides aloud to a wall.

The business case

Generated decks create a new risk: presenters who don't understand their own slides. Flipped turns Gemini in Slides from a shortcut into a coach — a reason for sales, education and enablement teams to pay for it.

Privacy by design

Speech is transcribed in the browser. The attention curve is a model of a room, not a recording of one — no audience data is collected.

How we'd prove it

Measure slides owned before and after, presenter confidence, and audience recall in a follow-up quiz — not minutes spent rehearsing.
