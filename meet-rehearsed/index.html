      “Isn't this just more prep work?”

      Rehearsal takes ninety seconds and runs off the invite you already wrote. One avoided follow-up meeting pays for a month of it.

      The business case

      Zoom and Teams are racing to the best recap. Meet can own the moment before the call — a Gemini for Workspace feature that admins can measure in hours saved.

      Privacy by design

      Risks describe the agenda, not people. Only the organizer sees the simulation. Only you see your mirror. Nobody gets a score they didn't ask for.

      How we'd prove it

      A/B test with organizers: decision rate, meetings ending on time, and follow-up meetings per decision — not just time spent in Meet.
