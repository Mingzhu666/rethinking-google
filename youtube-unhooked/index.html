“Doesn't this just cut watch time?”

In the short run, yes. But regret compounds into uninstalls, screen-time limits, and lost trust. Worth-it rate measures whether people are still here in ten years.

The business case

The anti-interest profile is a natural YouTube Premium differentiator: advertisers buy attention, members pay for control over it.

Regulatory tailwind

The EU's DSA requires very large platforms to offer a feed that isn't based on profiling. Blind box and time capsule are compliance that people actually want to use.

How we'd prove it

A/B test: the treatment group gets the anti-interest profile. Measure 90-day retention, weekly worth-it rate, and Premium conversion — not just daily watch time.
